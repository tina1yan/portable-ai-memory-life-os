# Changelog

## v0.1 — 2026-10-17
- Initial public conceptual research note.
- Seven explicitly anonymized retrospective case examples.
- Separates quota-pressure, project stock, maintenance, continuity failure and independent verification.
- Five candidate testable hypotheses and a measurement protocol.
- Limits: one practitioner, self-reported observations, no measured causal effect.
- The first public GitHub working paper was deposited to Zenodo on 2026-10-17, with version DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23439046](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23439046). Future substantive changes to archived manuscript require a new Zenodo version; the v0.1 citation remains fixed.

Future revisions should keep dated version history, identify evidence newly added, and avoid overwriting meaning of earlier claims without disclosure.
